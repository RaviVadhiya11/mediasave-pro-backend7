05 Help & FAQs

  
Can I download every public Reel, Short, Facebook video or Spotlight?
No. Public availability alone does not mean a video is downloadable. Use the platform's native download/export feature or save files you own or have permission to use. The Platform Link Guide explains the official options.

  
What is a direct media-file URL?
It is a link that points to a file such as MP4, WebM, MP3, JPG or PNG—not a profile, post page or normal webpage. MediaSave Pro checks the URL format; the original host decides whether the file can be opened or downloaded.

  
Can Video Tools create true 4K from a 720p video?
No. The 1080p, 2K and 4K choices are maximum output caps. The tool never upscales beyond the source resolution or invents missing detail. Processing runs on your device.

  
Why is there no download button for some social links?
A platform may limit downloads, omit media URLs, or require creator permission. MediaSave Pro shows the original post and official save steps instead of bypassing restrictions.
