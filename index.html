GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can I download any public Instagram Reel?

No. Public visibility does not automatically grant download rights or an official media URL. Use Instagram’s own Download option when available, or download your own authorised media from the private creator page.

Why does a social post link not download?

A post URL is a webpage, not a direct MP4 file. Platforms control media access, permissions and download options. MediaSave Pro does not scrape pages or bypass those controls.

Does MediaSave Pro support 1080p, 2K and 4K?

The direct downloader preserves the source file. Browser conversion presets are maximum output sizes only, depend on your device and never upscale beyond the source dimensions.

Do I need to sign in or share my password?

No sign-in is required for direct-file checks. The private creator page uses a separate owner access key for the site owner’s authorised Instagram account. Never share that key.

Are my files stored on MediaSave Pro?

Direct files are streamed to your browser and are not kept as a persistent MediaSave Pro archive. Recent activity is stored locally in your browser.

Why might a direct link fail?

The host may block server requests, require a session, redirect to another page, or return a file larger than the current limit. Try the source’s own download option.
