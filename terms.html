Terms of Use.

Rules for using MediaSave Pro's public tools.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Acceptable use

Use MediaSave Pro only for media you created, own, are licensed to use, or have clear permission to save and process. You are responsible for complying with copyright, privacy, publicity and other applicable laws and the source platform's terms.

2. Direct media links

The direct-link tool validates a supported file URL. When you choose Download file, the backend fetches the original file over HTTPS and streams it to your browser as an attachment. Supported media extensions only, public HTTPS hosts only, no redirects, and a 100 MB per-file limit apply. The service does not verify ownership or guarantee that a source host permits access. Use it only for files you own or have permission to save.

3. Social-media links

Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and other social post URLs are not treated as direct media files. Use each platform's own download/export features or a documented, authorized integration. MediaSave Pro does not scrape public accounts, bypass login walls, defeat DRM, or extract restricted streams. It shows official platform guidance for Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and Snapchat links.

4. Browser video tools

Video conversion and thumbnail creation are performed in the browser on your device. Support depends on your browser, device memory, codecs and file size. Output choices are maximum resolution caps (480p, 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 2160p) and never upscale beyond the source video dimensions. Selecting 4K does not create missing detail. WebM output is provided when supported; identical quality, size reduction or successful processing is not guaranteed.

5. Instagram creator area

The private Instagram area is for the site owner's authorized Professional account. Availability of media URLs is controlled by Meta and may vary by media, permissions, licensed audio and download settings. The area is not a public downloader for arbitrary accounts.

6. No affiliation

MediaSave Pro is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram, Meta, YouTube, Facebook or Google. Third-party names and logos belong to their respective owners.

7. Availability and changes

The service is provided on an "as available" basis. Features may change, become unavailable or be limited. Do not rely on this service as your only copy or backup of important media.

8. Misuse and reports

Do not use the service to infringe rights, harass others, distribute malware, or attempt to disrupt or abuse the service. To report a rights or safety concern, use the Contact page.
