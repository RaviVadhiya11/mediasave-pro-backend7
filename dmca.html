Copyright & removal requests

MediaSave Pro does not host a public library of downloaded media. Direct media is streamed from its source to the visitor and is not intentionally retained as a permanent archive.

How to submit a notice

If you believe a feature on this site infringes your rights, contact the site operator through the project's support route. A complete notice should identify:

  1. The copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  2. The exact URL or feature at issue.
  3. Your contact details and a good-faith statement that the use is not authorised.
  4. A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised agent.

Do not post private addresses, phone numbers, identity documents or other sensitive personal data in public GitHub issues. Request a private contact route through the repository support channel.
