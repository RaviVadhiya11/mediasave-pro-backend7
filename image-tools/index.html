QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Are these image tools really free?

The tools on this first version are free to use. No account or subscription is required. Browser and device limits may apply.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The image editing tools use your browser's local image and canvas capabilities. Your selected image files are not uploaded to our server.

Can I use PixelNest on Android?

Yes. The pages are designed to work on mobile browsers. Large images may need more memory, so close other tabs if your phone runs low on memory.

Can I enlarge a small photo to true 4K quality?

Normal resizing only changes pixel dimensions. It cannot recreate missing image detail. An AI upscaling engine is not included in this release.

Does the site keep my original images?

No. The tool creates a new downloadable copy. Your original file is not modified.
