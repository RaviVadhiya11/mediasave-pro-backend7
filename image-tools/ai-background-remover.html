HOW TO USE THIS AI TOOL

AI Background Remover in three steps

  1. Choose one clear portrait photo from your device.
  2. Select Transparent PNG, White background or a custom background colour.
  3. Press Remove background with AI. The first run downloads the model; when processing finishes, download the PNG or JPEG result.

How it works

Choose one portrait image. The browser downloads the MODNet model the first time, then runs inference on your phone. Transparent PNG is the default output. The model is designed for portrait/person matting and may struggle with busy backgrounds, hair detail or non-human objects.

AI processing runs in your browser using Transformers.js and model files hosted by Hugging Face. The selected photo is passed to the local browser model and is not uploaded to a PixelNest image-processing server. Model downloads require an internet connection. Very large images, older phones or low available memory can cause slow processing or an out-of-memory error.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for every object?

The selected MODNet model is designed for portrait/person matting. It may not reliably isolate products, pets, vehicles or complex groups.

Do I need internet?

Yes, the AI model must be downloaded the first time. After the model is cached, it may be reused by your browser.

Are photos uploaded?

The model runs in your browser. The image is not sent to a PixelNest processing server. Model files are downloaded from a public model host.

Why is it slow on my phone?

AI inference uses device memory and processing power. Try a smaller image and close other browser tabs.
